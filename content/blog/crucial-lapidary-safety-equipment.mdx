## The Hidden Dangers in the Dust

Gem cutting is a rewarding hobby, but the materials involved present significant health risks if not handled correctly.

### Silicosis
Almost all common stones (quartz, agate, jasper) contain silica. When ground, they produce microscopic dust. If inhaled, this dust causes silicosis, an incurable lung disease.
- **Rule #1**: Always cut wet. Water traps the dust before it goes airborne.
- **Rule #2**: Wear a proper respirator (N95 minimum, P100 preferred) even when cutting wet, as mist can carry particles.

### Eye and Ear Protection
Rock chips fracture like glass. Impact-resistant safety enclosures or safety glasses are mandatory. Furthermore, continuous exposure to diamond saws requires ear protection to prevent hearing loss. Stay safe, and lapidary will be a lifelong joy.
