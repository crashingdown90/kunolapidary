## Why Wire Wrapping?

Creating a beautiful cabochon is only half the battle. Unless you have metalsmithing skills, turning that stone into wearable art can be challenging. Wire wrapping is an accessible and elegant solution.

### Essential Tools
- **Flush Cutters**: To ensure clean wire ends.
- **Round-Nose Pliers**: To create loops and curves.
- **Flat-Nose Pliers**: For gripping and straightening wire.

Getting started with a simple pendant wrap using dead-soft copper wire is the best way to practice securing your stone without obscuring its natural beauty.
