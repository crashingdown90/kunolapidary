## Are Treated Gems Real?

When entering the world of colored gemstones, you will quickly encounter the term \"treatment.\" A treated gem is a natural stone that has undergone artificial processes to enhance its appearance or durability.

### Common Treatments
1. **Heat Treatment**: The most common and accepted enhancement. It improves color and clarity, especially in rubies, sapphires, and citrine.
2. **Irradiation**: Exposing minerals to radiation to alter their color centers. This is how almost all blue topaz gets its signature hue.
3. **Fracture Filling**: Using oils, resins, or glass to hide surface-reaching cracks. This is heavily utilized in emeralds.

While treatments are not inherently bad, ethical sellers must always disclose them to buyers.
